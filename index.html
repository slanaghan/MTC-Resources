MATHEMATICS IS BETTER TOGETHER

A circle for curious teachers.

Since 2015, the CSUDH Math Teachers’ Circle has brought K–12 teachers and university faculty together to explore mathematics through collaborative problem solving.

With support from the California Mathematics Project, our summer institutes offer more time to investigate mathematical ideas and connect them to teaching and learning. This library brings those explorations and their materials together.
